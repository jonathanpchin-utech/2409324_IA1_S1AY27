Three Important Parts of My Personality

1. Curiosity

I like understanding why things work instead of only memorising answers. This encourages me to ask questions and look for examples when I am learning a difficult topic. In computing, it helps me stay interested when I have to work through a problem.

2. Determination

I care about my future and want to keep improving. This pushes me to continue when schoolwork becomes difficult. I also need to turn that determination into consistent study habits, because wanting a good result is not enough on its own.

3. Faith

My Catholic faith is an important part of my life. It reminds me to be honest, patient and considerate of other people. At school, I want these values to influence how I treat people and how responsibly I approach my work.

Someone I Look Up To

Saint Thomas Aquinas

Saint Thomas Aquinas, a Dominican friar, philosopher and theologian who lived in the thirteenth century. He is known for his writings on Catholic theology, especially the Summa Theologiae. I admire his use of careful reasoning to explore questions about God and his belief that faith and reason work together.

"Believing is an act of the intellect assenting to the divine truth by command of the will moved by God through grace."

This teaching from Benedict XVI’s audience on St Thomas Aquinas This quote explains that believing involves both the mind and the will, with the help of God’s grace. It stands out to me because I want to understand my faith better and be able to explain what I believe. His approach encourages me to keep learning and asking questions.

LLMs: A Tool, Not a Crutch

LLMs To me are tools that help me learn. Using it as a crutch means depending on it to do work that I cannot explain myself.

LLMS no doubt can assist in many ways unforseen before when used correctly. they can scrape the web finding niche resources that no one would otherwise think of, they can do repetive simple tasks quicky that would otherwise waste human time and resources, And they can simplyify complex issues down to a individuals personal understanding

There are also problems with relying on these tools. An answer can sound convincing and still be wrong. Copying an answer can hide a gap in my understanding. In these times it is very important we do not oursource human thinking and ingenuity to these tools who at most come up with answers that already exist. It is also important we remember that the arts for our enjoyment and creativity. and not forget the soul in matters for the sake of profit and effeciency

As students, We should check important claims against reliable sources and always keep human persons in mind Saving time does not help if our goals are to complete everything with speed and effeciency but have not developed or learned from our experencies.
